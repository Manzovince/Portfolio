Topography

A generator of procedural topographic maps: contour lines drawn from noise, animated, with palettes, layered fills, and exports to PNG, SVG and video.

Open Topography

Contour lines make a beautiful pattern on their own, far from any map. Topography draws endless terrains of them, to use as backgrounds or posters, or just to watch them move.

From noise to lines

Each map starts from a seed. Layers of simplex noise build a height field, which is shaped into hills, ridges, terraces or islands, with an optional sea level. The marching squares algorithm then traces the contour lines through that field, and they are smoothed before being drawn.

The animation moves through the noise itself: the terrain slowly morphs, drifts across the screen, or keeps still while its lines flow uphill.

Styles and exports

Seven palettes, from paper to neon, a layered or gradient fill with hillshading, a pixelated render, and a randomise button for happy accidents. Every map can be saved as a PNG, a vector SVG, a WebM video of the animation, or even as ASCII text.
