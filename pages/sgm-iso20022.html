SG Markets ISO 20022

ISO 20022 is the new standard for payment messages between financial institutions. Its XML files are long and strict: one wrong tag and a payment is rejected.

Born from a design challenge, this service lets corporate clients and Societe Generale's own teams check their files against the standard before sending them, and understand exactly what to fix.

Process

We started from a short brief and a proof of concept: a page where you could upload a file and see it raw, with errors in red. It worked for experts, but the XML structure is hard to read for everyone else. We kept that view for advanced users and designed a simplified one, focused on finding and fixing errors quickly.

After an ideation phase, we chose a layout that mirrors the file itself: payment batches as tabs, transactions in a side panel, and the selected transaction laid out in the centre, block by block.

Then we tested every day. In short dailies with internal users, people who handle these files, we showed the latest version, watched them use it and adjusted the design before the next session. Wrong assumptions were caught within a day instead of at the end of the project.

Solution

The home page introduces the ISO 20022 standard, gives tips for a smooth migration, and offers an upload area for one or more files.

Files are parsed locally, in the browser, and displayed in a human-readable way: the errors only, or the whole file. Each error is highlighted where it occurs, with an explanation and a link to the section of the standard that tells how to fix it.

What I learned

Testing every day with the people who would use the tool turned testing into a habit rather than a phase. Small, frequent sessions kept the design grounded in real files and real errors, not in the specification alone.
