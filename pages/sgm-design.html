SG Markets Design

SG Markets Design is the website of Societe Generale's product design team. I migrated it to the latest version of our design system, and built the complete redesign of its Join us page: the page meant to make designers want to join the team.

Role
Designer and front-end developer
Team
The whole design team for the ideation, then me for the build
  • React
  • Git
Explore the site
Process

The redesign started at a team event, with the whole design team. We split into small groups, each one designing a specific section of the page, then ran rounds of votes across all the teams to keep the strongest ideas. The final page brings several of them together.

I then took the page from those ideas to production, with a focus on engaging interactions.

What I learned

The hardest part, for me, was the technology: I learned React on this project. Components, a responsive layout and CSS animations, then everything needed to ship: the deployment configuration, and an audit after release to keep the page fast.

It gave me a much better sense of what front-end developers deal with every day, and made our conversations easier ever since.

Outcome

The new page was well received: it was complimented on social networks, and the team saw spontaneous applications go up after it went live.

To see it in motion, visit SG Markets Design.
