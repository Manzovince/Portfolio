Life calendar

A whole life drawn as a grid of tiny squares, one for each week. Enter a date of birth, and the weeks already lived fill in, up to the one happening right now.

    Design
    Dev
Open Life calendar

The idea comes from Tim Urban's "Your Life in Weeks": ninety rows of fifty-two squares fit on a single page, and seeing them all at once changes how a week feels. I wanted my own version, one I could fill with the chapters of my life.

Chapters and moments

Chapters colour a stretch of squares: school, a city, a job, still going on or long finished. Moments circle a single square, for the dates worth remembering. Hovering a square tells its age and dates, and the grid can switch to months or years for a calmer view.

Everything stays in the browser, and the calendar prints as a poster.
