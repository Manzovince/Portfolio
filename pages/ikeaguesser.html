Everyone has stood in a showroom wondering what on earth a FJÄLLBO is. This quiz grew out of that joke, and it was vibe-coded without much of a plan: describe a game mode, try it, keep what is fun.

Six ways to play

Guess the object behind a Swedish name, or the name behind an object. Tell real products from invented ones that sound just as Swedish. Translate names with a little dictionary (BLÅHAJ means blue shark). Work out the naming code: why beds get Norwegian place names and rugs Danish ones. Or mix everything in a 60-second race against the clock.

Each round of ten questions ends with a rank, from lost in the showroom to flat-pack master.

Take it lightly

The names, translations and fun facts were written quickly and have not all been checked against the catalogue, so take the trivia with a pinch of salt. This is a fan quiz, not affiliated with IKEA; the drawings are simple pictograms, not product photos.
