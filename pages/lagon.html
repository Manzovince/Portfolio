Lagon

A living poster of light on water: caustics rippling across a pool floor, in step with the real time of day. Golden at sunrise, bright blue at noon, pink at dusk and almost black at night.

Open Lagon

Lagon is meant to stay open on a spare screen or a tablet, like a window onto a swimming pool. It reads the clock and nothing else: no buttons, no sound, just the water changing slowly with the day.

Light through the day

The day is described by a dozen key moments, each with its own water tint, light colour and caustic strength, from deep night to the two golden hours. The page blends between them minute by minute.

The sun travels from east to west, and the lower it is, the more the pattern stretches in its direction, like real light grazing the water.

A screen-printed look

Everything is drawn by a single WebGL shader. The caustics are thin ridges in warped noise, flattened into a few tones like a screen print, with a slight colour split and some grain. It renders below the screen's resolution, which keeps the image soft and phones cool.

Add ?debug to the address, or press D, to scrub through the day.
